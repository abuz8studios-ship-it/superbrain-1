# AHMAD-1 — What It Is

**One brain. One project.**
All prior names (MUTHALLATH-1, triangle model, geometric AI, Arabic root system) are retired.
Everything about the brain lives here.

## The product
A personal sovereign reasoning brain trained on Ahmad Odeh's worldview.
Small, specialist, edge-deployable. Runs on mobile, Android, Linux, MacBook.
Target: publish on HuggingFace. Gets Ahmad hired and noticed.

## What makes it different from every other model
- Reasons in triangles, fractals, and Arabic roots — not flat token prediction
- Five-sense embeddings (color, smell, sound, texture, frequency as geometry)
- ISNAD verifier — every claim must be traceable, graded 0-3, only grade 2+ enters training
- NeverStopLoop — refuses to give up, retries up to 5 times, never says "I can't"
- Trained on Ahmad's own 2,582 session transcripts — learns HIS reasoning patterns
- Understands the Quran at root-word level, not translation level

## What it is NOT
- Not a general knowledge dump
- Not a 70B+ parameter monster
- Not dependent on cloud APIs to function
- Not another fine-tune of an existing model with a different system prompt

## Done-test (one sentence)
Ask it a hard question in Ahmad's domain. It traces the answer to first principles,
uses geometric/frequency/root reasoning, cites its chain, and never halts with "I don't know."
